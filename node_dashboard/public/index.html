💡 Mission Intelligence & Physics Guide

1. 📡 Layer 1: RF Communications & FHSS Hopping

The hostile drone communicates using Frequency Hopping Spread Spectrum (FHSS) across 50+ pseudorandom radio channels to resist signal interception and single-frequency spot jamming.

2. ⚡ Layer 1: Electronic Warfare (EW) Jamming

Defensive electronic countermeasures disrupt telemetry. Barrage spreads power uniformly across the band, Sweep cycles across frequencies at high speed, and Reactive cognitively locks onto active hops within dwell time.

3. 🛰️ Layer 2: Free-Space Path Loss & RSSI Ranging

Passive sensor nodes measure Received Signal Strength ($RSSI$). Distance is derived via ITU Free-Space Path Loss: $RSSI = P_{tx} - 20\log_{10}(d) - 20\log_{10}(f) - 32.44 + \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$.

4. 🎯 Layer 2: Levenberg-Marquardt Multilateration

Using 3+ sensor range measurements, a non-linear least-squares optimization computes the drone's position fix $(X, Y)$ and evaluates Geometric Dilution of Precision (GDOP) to assess localization confidence.

5. 🔮 AI Trajectory Prediction & EKF Geofencing

The Constant Turn Rate and Velocity (CTRV) state estimator projects the drone's path forward up to 10 seconds, computing a dynamic 95% uncertainty cone and predicting boundary breaches in advance.
